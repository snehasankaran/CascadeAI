## The 7 Changes That Move CascadeAI from 4th to 1st

### Change 1: Reframe the Narrative
- OLD: "CascadeAI is a predictive system with a novel graph-based architecture"
- NEW: "318 million people face crisis-level hunger TODAY. Sudan, Hormuz, Ukraine — cascades keep happening, responses keep coming too late. CascadeAI closes the 120-day gap."
- Open with PRESENT-TENSE crisis (318M, Sudan, Hormuz +46%), validate with Ukraine 2022 backtest, close with callback to present
- **Community-first ethos:** Institutions get technical briefings. Communities get the same early warning — in their native language. A mother in Turkana gets Swahili. A farmer in Bangladesh gets Bengali. Empowerment, not dependency.

### Change 2: Reduce Scope
- KEEP (P1): Replay Engine (D1), Compound BFS (D2), Audience Narratives with native language (D6)
- DEMOTE (P3): Intervention Optimizer (D3), Crisis Fingerprinting (D4)
- CUT: Living Graph (D5)
- 3 polished features > 6 half-finished features
- D6 now has **6 audience profiles** including Community Preparedness Alert (native language) and Public Awareness Brief

### Change 3: Differentiate from HungerMap LIVE and CERES + Use 2026 Crises
- HungerMap/CERES predict food insecurity (single domain, observation-based)
- CascadeAI predicts cross-domain cascades (scenario-based, multiple domains)
- Video line: "Existing tools predict hunger. CascadeAI predicts the cascade that causes hunger."
- NEW: Live validation — Hormuz fertilizer surge (+46%) confirms the ENERGY→FERTILIZER edge
- NEW: Sudan cascade (war→displacement→food→health→water) confirms the full graph model
- NEW: Open video with "318 million people TODAY" not just "47 million in 2022"

### Change 4: Find Real-World Validation
- Email 5-10 humanitarian analysts (WFP, OCHA, FEWS NET)
- Reference OCHA Anticipatory Action framework
- Frame HungerMap LIVE as validation, not competition

### Change 5: Restructure the Video
- OLD: Problem → Solution → Hypothetical demo → Backtest → Accessibility → Close
- NEW: Hook (real footage) → 120-day gap → Promise → BACKTEST DEMO FIRST → Future scenario → Offline → Close
- Backtest first = real data, not fiction. More powerful, more credible.

### Change 6: Invest 3 Days in Video (Not 1)
- May 12: Script + rehearse + gather footage
- May 13: Record (5+ takes) + voiceover
- May 14: Edit (captions, music, transitions)
- May 15: Upload to YouTube (buffer for processing)

### Change 7: Rewrite the Writeup
- Structure: Problem (300w) → Solution (400w) → Proof (300w) → Gemma 4 (300w) → Impact (200w)
- Story-first, not architecture-first
