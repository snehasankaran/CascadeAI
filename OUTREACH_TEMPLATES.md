## Template 2: Email to OCHA/WFP Data Team (Detailed)

Subject: Open-source AI tool for cross-domain crisis cascade prediction — seeking feedback

Dear [Name / Team],

I am developing CascadeAI, an open-source humanitarian early warning tool
for the Gemma 4 Good Hackathon (Kaggle/Google DeepMind). The tool predicts
how a single crisis event cascades through interconnected global systems:

- A conflict disrupts energy → energy affects fertilizer supply → fertilizer
  impacts crop yields → crop yields drive food prices → food prices cause
  malnutrition → malnutrition increases displacement

Unlike single-domain prediction tools, CascadeAI models the cross-domain
cascade using a researched dependency graph (18 edges, 11 nodes) combined
with AI agents powered by Google's Gemma 4 model.

Key features:
- Backtested against Ukraine 2022 (predicted 40-55% wheat price increase
  vs actual 53%)
- Per-country impact predictions for 8 vulnerable nations
- Stakeholder-specific response plans (WHO, WFP, UNHCR)
- Runs offline on consumer hardware for field workers

I believe this aligns with OCHA's Anticipatory Action framework and the
growing need for integrated early warning systems.

Would you be willing to provide a brief perspective on whether a tool like
this would be useful for anticipatory action planning? Even a 1-2 sentence
response would be incredibly valuable.

I am happy to share the demo or discuss the methodology further.

Thank you for your time and for the critical work you do.

Best regards,
[Your name]
[Your affiliation if relevant]
[Link to GitHub repo when available]
