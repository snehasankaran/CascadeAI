## RECORDING NOTES

### What to Record Separately
1. **Screen recording** (OBS): The full Streamlit demo, rehearsed and clean
2. **Voiceover** (separate mic recording): Read the script naturally, not rushed
3. **Ollama terminal** (OBS): Short clip of Ollama running + offline dashboard
4. **CC footage** (pre-downloaded): Opening food crisis footage from Pexels

### Editing Assembly Order
1. Open with CC footage + voiceover (Scene 1-2)
2. Transition to dependency graph animation (Scene 3)
3. Main screen recording with voiceover overlay (Scene 4-5)
4. Split screen for Ollama (Scene 6)
5. Final cascade map + end card (Scene 7)

### Key Editing Tips
- **Background music at 10-15% volume** — audible but doesn't compete with voice
- **Hard cuts, no fancy transitions** — clean and professional
- **Zoom in on key numbers** when showing backtest comparison
- **2-second pauses** after the backtest reveal numbers — let it sink in
- **Captions burned in** — judges may watch on mute
- **1080p minimum** — YouTube will process higher resolution better
- **Export as MP4, H.264 codec** — universal compatibility

### Narrative Arc Summary
- **Open:** Present-tense crisis (318M, Sudan, Hormuz) — "this is happening NOW"
- **Middle:** Prove it works on Ukraine 2022 — "we validated against real history"
- **Twist:** Live validation — "our model predicted what actually happened in 2026"
- **Close:** Callback to opening — "318M people are hungry because cascades move faster than our response"

This structure is a LOOP — opens with today's crisis, closes with today's crisis.
The Ukraine backtest in the middle provides the credibility bridge.
